# Finance Suite Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

## Overview

Finance Suite is a personal, non-commercial financial management application used by its owner to consolidate and analyse information from the owner's own financial accounts.

## Data processed

Finance Suite may process account information, balances, transaction history, transaction descriptions, dates, amounts, currencies and other information made available by financial institutions after the account holder has explicitly authorised access.

## Open banking access

Where Enable Banking is used, access to financial account information is performed through the user's explicit authorisation and the applicable bank authentication and strong customer authentication process. Finance Suite does not request or store the user's online banking password for Enable Banking connections.

## Purpose

Financial data is processed solely to provide personal financial management functionality, including consolidation of accounts, categorisation of income and expenses, analysis, dashboards and personal reports.

## Data sharing

Finance Suite is not offered as a service to third parties. Financial data is not sold or used for advertising. Data is shared with service providers only where technically necessary to provide an explicitly requested integration.

## Storage and security

The application owner controls the Finance Suite deployment and storage. Application credentials, private keys and other secrets must be stored outside source control using appropriate environment variables or secret-management mechanisms.

## Data retention and deletion

Financial data is retained only for the owner's personal financial management purposes. The owner may remove imported data, disconnect integrations or revoke bank consent at any time using the relevant application or financial institution controls.

## Contact

For privacy or data-protection questions, use the contact email registered for the Finance Suite application with Enable Banking.
