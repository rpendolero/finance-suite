# Finance Suite Terms of Use

Last updated: 7 October 2026

## Purpose

Finance Suite is a personal, non-commercial financial management application intended for use by its owner with the owner's own financial accounts.

## Personal use only

The application is not a commercial banking, payment, accounting or financial-advisory service and is not offered to third parties. It must not be used to access financial accounts without the account holder's authorisation.

## Open banking integrations

Finance Suite may use Enable Banking and participating financial institutions to retrieve account information after explicit user authorisation. Availability, supported account types, transaction history and consent duration depend on the relevant financial institution and integration provider.

## No payment initiation

Finance Suite's open-banking integration is intended for account-information and personal financial-analysis purposes. It does not initiate payments unless that functionality is separately implemented, explicitly disclosed and appropriately authorised.

## Accuracy

Finance Suite presents and analyses information obtained from financial institutions and imported statements. Such information may be delayed, incomplete or subject to correction. Official bank records remain the authoritative source for account balances and transactions.

## Security

Users are responsible for protecting application credentials, private keys and access to the systems on which Finance Suite is deployed. Secrets must not be committed to public source-control repositories.

## Changes

These terms may be updated as Finance Suite evolves or its integrations change.

## Contact

Questions regarding these terms can be sent to the contact email registered for the Finance Suite application with Enable Banking.
